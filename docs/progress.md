Replaced Hugging Face `AutoModel(trust_remote_code=True)` (incompatible with `transformers` v5 due to missing `all_tied_weights_keys`) with an exact PyTorch `MiewIDBackbone` loader verified `strict=True` against all 1,208 official `model.safetensors` tensors (`GeM p=3.000`).
  - **H1 (Validation):** Random split inflated Rank-1 (`MiewID-msv3`: 93.2% random vs. 75.0% unseen days, -18.2 pp; `MegaDescriptor-L-384`: 92.5% vs. 81.8%, -10.7 pp). Cross-camera vs. same-camera dropped Rank-1 by 15.4 pp for `MiewID-msv3` (70.9% -> 55.5%) and 9.1 pp for `MegaDescriptor-L-384` (77.9% -> 68.8%).
  - **H2 (Validation):** `crop70` caused the largest drop (`MiewID`: -23.8 pp; `MegaDescriptor`: -33.2 pp), followed by blur (`MiewID`: -6.7 pp; `MegaDescriptor`: -9.0 pp). `low_light` (0.35x) slightly helped `MiewID-msv3` on validation (+5.3 pp), likely due to bright overhead barn lighting (mean pixel brightness 158.6/255).
  - **H3 (Validation):** `id_margin` outperformed `top1_sim` on closed-set error detection (`MiewID`: 0.887 vs. 0.783; `MegaDescriptor`: 0.892 vs. 0.785) and open-set unknown detection (`MiewID`: 0.751 vs. 0.706; `MegaDescriptor`: 0.785 vs. 0.713). Locked `id_margin` for both rules before running the test set.
  - **H4 (Validation):** Gallery-only residual adapter on frozen `MiewID-msv3` lifted cross-camera validation Rank-1 from 55.5% to 83.2% (+27.6 pp), cross-day Rank-1 from 75.0% to 83.2% (mAP 38.5% -> 84.7%), and open-set unknown AUROC from 0.751 to 0.832.
- **22.11.2026 (Step 03 — Locked Held-Out Test Evaluation, run once):** Completed cleanly on Kaggle Tesla T4 (`veliates/miewid-reid-03-test-set`). Results and figures saved to `results/`.
  - **H1 (Test):** `MiewID-msv3` dropped from 93.2% (random split) to 87.7% on unseen test days 19–20 (-5.6 pp; smaller than on days 17–18), and from 80.4% (same camera) to 66.6% (cross camera, **-13.9 pp**). On wild `NyalaData` test, `MiewID-msv3` reached **93.2%** same-side vs. **79.8%** opposite-side (**-13.3 pp** viewpoint drop), far ahead of `MegaDescriptor-L-384` (49.3% same-side / 47.8% opposite-side) even though `MegaDescriptor` led on indoor barn cows (90.4% vs. 87.7%).
  - **H2 (Test):** `MiewID-msv3` dropped -4.2 pp under `low_light` (83.5%), **-22.9 pp** under `blur` (64.8%), and **-26.9 pp** under `crop70` (60.8%). `MegaDescriptor-L-384` resisted blur better (-7.4 pp to 83.1%) but collapsed harder under `crop70` (**-35.1 pp** to 55.3%).
  - **H3 (Test):** Locked `id_margin` clearly beat `top1_sim` for closed-set error detection (`MiewID`: **0.911** vs. 0.880; `MegaDescriptor`: **0.919** vs. 0.857); abstaining on the lowest-margin 20% lifted `MiewID-msv3` Rank-1 from 87.7% to **96.2%**. On open-set unknown detection (10 unseen test cows, `n=83`), locked `id_margin` reached **0.853** AUROC on `MiewID-msv3` (**0.894** adapted; **0.850** on `MegaDescriptor`), though raw `top1_sim` actually scored higher on `MiewID-msv3` (`0.900`, adapted `0.925`, 51.8% unknown TPR at 5% FPR).
  - **H4 (Test):** Gallery-only residual adapter lifted frozen `MiewID-msv3` cross-camera Rank-1 from **66.6% to 87.0% (+20.4 pp)** and doubled cross-camera mAP (30.1% -> 60.5%), shrinking the same-camera vs. cross-camera gap from 13.9 pp to **0.4 pp** (87.4% vs. 87.0%). All-camera cross-day Rank-1 stayed flat (87.7% -> 87.0%) while all-camera mAP jumped from 46.8% to **88.1%**, blur Rank-1 rose from 64.8% to 73.1%, and open-set unknown AUROC improved on both metrics (`id_margin`: 0.853 -> 0.894; `top1_sim`: 0.900 -> 0.925). Minor label overlap in `results/fig2_degradations.png` adjusted locally from `results/test_summary.json` without changing any numbers.
